\# Pseudocode for Data Analysis



\## Step 1: Load the Dataset

\- Locate the dataset and load it into the analysis environment.

\- Examine the dataset to understand the variables, data types, and number of observations.



\## Step 2: Clean the Data

\- Check the data for missing values, duplicate records, and incorrect or invalid entries.

\- Remove duplicate records and determine how missing values should be handled.

\- Check that each variable is in the appropriate format and look for unusual or extreme values.



\## Step 3: Calculate Summary Statistics

\- Calculate relevant summary statistics such as counts, means, medians, minimums, maximums, and standard deviations.

\- Review the results to identify patterns, differences, or other notable characteristics in the data.



\## Step 4: Create a Visualization

\- Choose a visualization that best represents the variables and relationships being examined.

\- Create the chart and include appropriate labels, titles, and other necessary information.



\## Step 5: Interpret the Results

\- Examine the summary statistics and visualization to identify important findings or relationships.

\- Summarize the results and consider any limitations or factors that could affect the analysis.
